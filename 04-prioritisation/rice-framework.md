# RICE Prioritisation

RICE score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort.

- **Reach:** number of users or events affected in a defined period.
- **Impact:** agreed impact scale; document the scale used.
- **Confidence:** evidence-based confidence percentage.
- **Effort:** person-months or another consistent effort unit.

| Simplify appointment search | 600 | 1.5 | 70% | 1.5 | 420 | Illustrative inputs only |
