# Product Experimentation & Growth — Toolkit

This folder contains reusable experiment-planning and analysis frameworks. They demonstrate a structured approach to testing product assumptions; they are **not evidence that a specific A/B test or growth experiment was run**.

Use experiments to test a defined uncertainty, not to manufacture justification for a preferred feature.

## Contents

- [Experiment brief](experiment-brief.md)
- [A/B test analysis](ab-test-analysis.md)
- [Growth loop canvas](growth-loop-canvas.md)

## Evidence standard

When adding a real experiment, record the hypothesis, target segment, primary and guardrail metrics, sample and duration assumptions, decision rule, observed results and limitations. Clearly distinguish simulated or hypothetical data from measured production results.
