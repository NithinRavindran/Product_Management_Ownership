# User Interview Guide

## Introduction
Explain the purpose, duration, voluntary nature of participation, consent and note-taking approach.

## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you tried to [task].
2. What triggered the need?
3. Walk me through what you did.
4. Where, if anywhere, did you get stuck?
5. What did you do next?
6. What alternatives or workarounds did you try?
7. What mattered most when deciding?
8. What would a better outcome look like?

## Avoid
- Leading questions.
- Asking participants to predict hypothetical future behaviour as the only evidence.
- Treating one participant's opinion as representative of all users.

## Notes
Capture observed behaviour, exact language with consent, context and follow-up questions separately from interpretation.
