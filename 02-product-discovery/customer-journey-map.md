# Customer Journey Map

| Stage | User goal | Actions | Questions / emotions | Friction | Opportunity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Understand options | Search / browse | What is available? | Unclear information | Improve comparison | Research needed |
| Evaluate | Choose confidently | Compare / review | Can I trust this? | Hidden constraints | Explain trade-offs | Research needed |
| Commit | Complete task | Enter details / confirm | Is this correct? | Form or payment friction | Simplify critical steps | Funnel data |
| Use | Receive value | Use service | What happens next? | Poor status visibility | Proactive updates | Support themes |
| Return | Repeat / recommend | Revisit | Is this easier next time? | Repeated effort | Remember preferences safely | Retention data |
