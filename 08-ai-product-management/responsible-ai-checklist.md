# Responsible AI Checklist

- [ ] User is informed when interacting with AI where appropriate.
- [ ] Intended use and prohibited use are documented.
- [ ] Sensitive data is minimised and protected.
- [ ] Data sources and rights are understood.
- [ ] Access controls apply to retrieval and tools.
- [ ] Outputs are evaluated for accuracy, bias and safety.
- [ ] Uncertainty and limitations are communicated.
- [ ] Human oversight exists for consequential decisions.
- [ ] Monitoring, feedback and incident handling are defined.
- [ ] Users can report errors and obtain support.
- [ ] Changes to models, prompts and datasets are versioned.
