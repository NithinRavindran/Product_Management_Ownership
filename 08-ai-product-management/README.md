# AI Product Management — Learning Lab

This folder contains learning notes, reusable frameworks and illustrative product artefacts for exploring AI-enabled product experiences. It is a **learning and portfolio-development area**, separate from the four professional product-domain case studies in [10-case-studies](../10-case-studies/).

The presence of an AI concept or template here should not be interpreted as a claim that the feature was launched in production or delivered for an employer. The [AI Travel Assistant](../10-case-studies/ai-travel-assistant.md), for example, is explicitly an illustrative concept.

## How to use these materials

Treat templates as starting points. For a credible AI product proposal, document the user problem, why AI is appropriate, non-AI alternatives, data and privacy constraints, failure modes, human oversight, evaluation criteria, operating cost and measurable user outcomes.
