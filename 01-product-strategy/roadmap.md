# Outcome-based Product Roadmap

This is an illustrative roadmap format, not a commitment to dates or delivery.

| Horizon | Desired outcome | Candidate initiatives | Dependencies | Evidence / open questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Now | Reduce friction in the core journey | Diagnose funnel drop-offs; simplify critical steps | Analytics, design, engineering | Establish baseline first |
| Next | Improve reliability and transparency | Better status visibility; integration monitoring | Supplier / platform dependencies | Validate highest-impact failure modes |
| Later | Enable intelligent assistance | Context-aware recommendations or assistant | Data quality, AI evaluation, privacy review | Test user value and safety |

## Roadmap hygiene
- Prioritise outcomes, not a list of promised features.
- Show confidence and dependencies.
- Explain why work is included and what is excluded.
- Update the roadmap when evidence changes.
