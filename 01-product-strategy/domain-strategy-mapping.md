# Strategy Mapping Across Four Product Domains

This document is a strategy working aid showing how common product-strategy tools can be applied to the four domains in this portfolio. It contains example questions and outcome areas, not validated market findings or commitments.

## 1. Corporate Travel Management Platform

**Strategic intent:** Make business travel easier to plan and manage while improving policy compliance, booking reliability and financial visibility.

- Customer segments: travellers, arrangers, approvers, travel managers and finance.
- Value proposition: connected request-to-trip experience with transparent policy and spend controls.
- Potential strategic themes: policy-aware booking, multi-city / international travel, mobile trip management, supplier reliability and spend intelligence.
- Candidate outcomes: request completion, approval turnaround, booking success, policy compliance, traveller satisfaction and forecast accuracy.
- Strategic tensions: traveller choice vs policy control; supplier breadth vs reliability; forecast detail vs data quality.

## 2. B2B & B2C Travel Technology Platform

**Strategic intent:** Enable travel partners and end customers to discover, compare and book multi-product travel experiences reliably.

- Customer segments: direct travellers, travel agents, agencies, partners and suppliers.
- Value proposition: broad, comparable travel inventory with reliable booking and servicing.
- Potential strategic themes: product breadth, supplier connectivity, channel-specific commercial models, conversion optimisation and dynamic packaging.
- Candidate outcomes: search-to-book conversion, supplier success, agency activation, repeat booking, package completion and support contacts per booking.
- Strategic tensions: inventory breadth vs search speed; commercial flexibility vs pricing consistency; package variety vs partial booking risk.

## 3. Healthcare Information System — HIMS, EHR & Laboratory Integration

**Strategic intent:** Support connected, reliable hospital and laboratory workflows while maintaining data integrity, privacy and traceability.

- Customer segments: clinicians, nursing teams, laboratory teams, hospital administrators and IT / integration teams.
- Value proposition: coherent patient, encounter, order and result workflows across authorised systems.
- Potential strategic themes: workflow digitisation, interoperability, identity integrity, device connectivity, result validation and operational visibility.
- Candidate outcomes: workflow completion, order-to-result turnaround, interface success, unmatched result rate, exception backlog and system availability.
- Strategic tensions: workflow speed vs validation; integration breadth vs site-specific complexity; data access vs privacy and role restrictions.

## 4. Vulnerability & Incident Management System

**Strategic intent:** Help security teams convert vulnerability findings and security signals into prioritised, owned and measurable remediation work.

- Customer segments: security analysts, incident responders, vulnerability managers, asset owners and security leaders.
- Value proposition: connected visibility from scanner finding and alert through incident triage and remediation verification.
- Potential strategic themes: scan coverage, finding quality, asset context, explainable correlation, lifecycle ownership and risk analytics.
- Candidate outcomes: finding freshness, time to triage, actionable alert conversion, SLA compliance, mean time to remediate and reopen rate.
- Strategic tensions: alert sensitivity vs noise; automation vs explainability; prioritisation speed vs context quality.

## Strategy validation checklist

- [ ] Product problem is supported by user or business evidence.
- [ ] Target segments and stakeholders are explicit.
- [ ] Value proposition is differentiated and testable.
- [ ] Strategic choices and non-goals are documented.
- [ ] Outcomes have metric definitions and data sources.
- [ ] Risks, dependencies and constraints are visible.
- [ ] Roadmap themes align with strategy and capacity.
- [ ] Assumptions have owners and validation plans.
