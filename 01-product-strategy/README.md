This section demonstrates how to connect customer problems, business goals and product investment decisions.

## Working principles
- Start with an evidenced customer or business problem.
- Express outcomes rather than only feature outputs.
- Make strategic choices and non-goals explicit.
- Revisit assumptions as new evidence becomes available.
