# Product Strategy

Product strategy connects customer needs, market context, organisational goals and investment choices. In this portfolio, the strategy toolkit is applied to four product domains: Corporate Travel Management, B2B & B2C Travel Technology, Healthcare Systems and Vulnerability & Incident Management.

## Strategy questions by domain

| Product domain | Strategic questions |
|---|---|
| Corporate Travel | How can the product improve traveller experience, policy compliance, booking reliability and spend visibility? |
| B2B & B2C Travel Tech | How can the product grow channel adoption, improve conversion, expand supply and maintain reliable booking? |
| Healthcare Systems | How can the product improve workflow efficiency, interoperability, data integrity and safe information access? |
| Vulnerability & Incident Management | How can the product improve signal quality, risk prioritisation, incident response and remediation outcomes? |

## Working principles
- Start with an evidenced customer or business problem.
- Understand market context, alternatives and strategic constraints.
- Express outcomes rather than only feature outputs.
- Connect product choices to business model and organisational strategy.
- Make strategic choices, trade-offs and non-goals explicit.
- Revisit assumptions as new evidence becomes available.
