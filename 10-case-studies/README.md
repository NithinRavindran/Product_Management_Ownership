# Product Case Studies

The case studies in this folder are **illustrative portfolio exercises** inspired by common product challenges in these domains. They are not representations of confidential employer projects, verified launches or independently measured outcomes. Replace hypotheses with research and real data before presenting a case study as completed work.

## Case study structure
Problem → users → evidence → opportunity → options → prioritisation → requirements → delivery → measurement → learnings.
